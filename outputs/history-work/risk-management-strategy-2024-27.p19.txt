Positive and Negative amendments are made regularly to risks in light of the above
to respond to the risk and continually update risk registers.

The general impression of risks
is that it is a negative event;
however it is also possible that
positive events and
opportunities can arise and the
risk score then becomes the
reverse of the rating for a
negative risk, e.g. an opportunity
with a high rating could deliver a
good return for little effort.

In managing a
negative risk
(Threat), we are
aiming to see the
risk rating
decrease so that
the likelihood and
consequence of
the risk decreases
should it
materialise.

A positive event
or opportunity is
measured in a
similar way to a
negative risk,
but the desired
direction of
travel is
reversed.
