Purpose of the Report

1.1 This report is the Council’s Risk Management Strategy for the future. It is important that
the Council has an overarching strategy for how it manages risk; risk can never be
eradicated, but it is important that the Council is clear on how it proposes to manage
different risks. This report proposes a changed risk management matrix to a ‘5x5’ matrix
as well as a new Risk Appetite.

2

Recommendation

2.1 For the Executive to approve the Risk Management Strategy.
