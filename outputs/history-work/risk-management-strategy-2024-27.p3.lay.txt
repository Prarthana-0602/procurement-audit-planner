4    Executive Summary
4.1 The Strategy in appendix A is the new risk management strategy for the Council. It
    builds on the existing risk management framework and its key changes include:

     •   Adoption of a 5x5 risk management matrix to provide greater clarity of risk
         management and control

     •   A new risk appetite to articulate the levels of risks accepted by the Executive and
         provide a framework for officers to work within

5    Other options considered
5.1 The Council could keep its existing risk management strategy, though this would not
    include changes and improvements identified to further enhance the Council’s approach
    to risk management.

6.1 For the Executive to approve the Risk Management Strategy
